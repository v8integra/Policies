BinderVault Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. Applies to the BinderVault app for Windows.

The short version

  • Everything you put in BinderVault stays on your PC, locked with your password.
  • We never receive your information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • The app uses the internet for only two things: Windows checking your Microsoft Store purchase, and a one-time download of the optional AI helpers, only if you say yes. Nothing about you or your binder is ever uploaded.

Who we are

BinderVault is made by V8developing ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the app. If you have questions, contact us at [your contact email].

What BinderVault stores, and where

BinderVault is an emergency binder. You choose what to put in it, which can include health details, contacts, accounts, insurance, documents, photos, letters and final wishes. All of it is kept in a binder file on your own PC.

That file is encrypted with a key made from your password (Argon2id and AES-256-GCM, both widely used and well-tested standards). Without your password or your recovery key, the file can't be read by anyone, including us.

To work while your binder is locked, the app also keeps a few small files on your PC that are not encrypted. None of them hold your answers, documents or letters:

  • the names you give your binders, so the app can list them;
  • if you turn on reminders at Windows sign-in, a list of dates when a reminder is due, without saying what any date is for;
  • your display choices, such as text size and colors, and whether you chose to use the AI helpers.

What we collect

Nothing. BinderVault has no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud storage, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting of its own and no tracking of any kind. We have no server that receives your information, so we have none to share, sell or lose.

When BinderVault uses the internet

BinderVault works offline. It connects to the internet in only these two cases:

  • Your Microsoft Store purchase. Windows checks that your copy of BinderVault was bought from the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles this, along with the purchase itself, under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Microsoft may give us anonymous, combined statistics, such as how many people installed the app or how often it crashed. These never include anything from your binder.
  • The optional AI helpers, only if you turn them on. Saying yes downloads the AI model once, about 2.6 GB, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). This download sends nothing about you or your binder. Like any download, it shows your internet address to Hugging Face, under Hugging Face's privacy policy. If you say no, this never happens.

Apart from these, the app blocks its own network access.

The optional AI helpers

The AI helpers are off unless you choose them, and you can turn them off or remove them at any time in Settings. When they're on, the AI runs entirely on your PC. It is never sent your binder, and nothing it writes is saved until you check it and choose to save.

  • Fill in from a photo: Windows' built-in text recognition reads the photo on your PC. A photo you pick from your PC is read in memory and not kept, unless you add it to your Documents yourself.
  • Letters, obituary drafts and questions about your binder: the AI only sees what it needs, such as your notes or the relevant answers. It never sees answers you've hidden, PINs, passwords, full account numbers or your letters.

The AI only runs while your binder is unlocked, keeps no record of what it was asked, and stops when your binder locks.

Windows Hello

If you choose to unlock with Windows Hello, your face, fingerprint or PIN is checked by Windows on your PC. BinderVault never receives your fingerprint or face data. It only receives a yes from Windows, in the form of a key that unlocks your binder on that PC.

Notifications and the clipboard

Reminder notifications only say that something in your binder needs attention. They never say what it is or whose binder it is. If you copy a hidden value, such as an account number, BinderVault clears it from the clipboard after 60 seconds or when the binder locks.

Things you create and share

You decide where backups, family copies, printouts and PDFs go. Backups and family copies are encrypted, and a family copy needs its own password to open. Printouts and PDFs are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe. Once you give a file or printout to someone else, it is in their hands, so share only with people you trust.

Deleting your information

Because your information is only on your PC, you're always in control of it:

  • Anything you delete in BinderVault goes to Recently deleted for 30 days, then is removed for good. You can also remove it sooner.
  • You can remove a whole binder from your PC in the app's settings.
  • Uninstalling BinderVault from Windows can remove the binders kept inside the app. Backups you saved elsewhere are never touched, so keep one if you want your binder to survive.
  • You can delete the AI download at any time in Settings > AI helpers.

We can't delete, recover or reset your binder, because we never have it. If you lose both your password and your recovery key, the binder can't be opened, by design.

Children

BinderVault is meant for adults planning for their families. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Details about your children that you choose to record stay in your binder, on your PC.

Changes to this policy
